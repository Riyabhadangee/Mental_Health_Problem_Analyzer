# 🧠 Mental Health Survey 

This repository presents an **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** project on a Mental Health Survey conducted within the technology industry. The goal is to analyze survey responses, identify patterns related to mental health, and uncover factors that may influence employees' willingness to seek treatment and workplace support.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Mental health has become an increasingly important topic in today's workplace, particularly in the technology sector. This project explores survey data to better understand mental health trends, workplace attitudes, and factors associated with seeking professional help.

The analysis includes data cleaning, preprocessing, visualization, and statistical insights to answer key questions from the dataset.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze mental health trends among employees
- Understand workplace attitudes toward mental health
- Identify factors affecting treatment-seeking behavior
- Explore relationships between age, gender, family history, and mental health
- Generate meaningful visualizations and business insights

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains survey responses from individuals working in the technology industry.

It includes features such as:

- Age
- Gender
- Country
- Self-employed status
- Family history of mental illness
- Treatment history
- Work interference
- Benefits provided
- Care options
- Wellness programs
- Supervisor support
- Anonymity
- Remote work
- Company size

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Employees with a family history of mental illness are more likely to seek treatment.
- Workplace benefits and wellness programs positively influence treatment-seeking behavior.
- Company culture and supervisor support play a significant role in employee mental well-being.
- Many employees hesitate to discuss mental health due to concerns about workplace stigma.
- Mental health awareness varies across different organizations and countries.
